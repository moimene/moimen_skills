# Base structure (Bolivia-style) — EN

Use this 5-slide skeleton for new investment teasers. Keep tone executive, numeric, concise.

## 1) Cover
- Background: deep navy/charcoal with orange accent (see `assets/modelo-bolivia.pdf`).
- Elements: opportunity title (“[Area] – [Asset type] Opportunity”), date, “Private & Confidential” stamp, sponsor logo.
- Optional: short tagline, sector icon.

## 2) Executive summary
- Header: “Executive Summary – [Project]” + asset type subtitle.
- Left column “The Opportunity”: 3–5 bullets on investment angle, timing, strategy, permit/status.
- Right column “The Location”: mini map (2–3 landmarks) + bullets on access, submarket, zoning/licensing.
- “Asset” block (if relevant): permitted use, key specs.
- “Investment highlights”: 4–6 bullets equity story (contrarian, pricing, yield-on-cost, target tenant, financing, exit).
- Fast table (2 lines text + numbers). Suggested fields:
  - Land plot / Uses / Buildability (sqm GFA)
  - Total Investment (€psm GFA)
  - Net Exit Yield (%) and Exit Price (€psm)
  - ERV (€psm/month)
  - IRR (%), Multiple (x), Equity-in (€m), Profit (€m)
  - Footnotes: what CapEx includes; metrics levered/pre-promote.

## 3) Market update / thesis
- Title: “[City – Submarket] market update” + contrarian claim.
- Left: 3–4 thesis bullets (vacancy, pipeline, demand, yields, public plan akin to 22@).
- Right “Conclusions”: numbered 1–4, crisp (e.g., “1 CBD vacancy <3%”).
- Charts (use placeholders if no data):
  - Line/area: 22@ take-up vs total + vacancy %.
  - Stacked bars: absorption by grade (A/B+/B/C) or future availability.
  - Cite source (CBRE/C&W/JLL) and data year.

## 4) Comparable / mirror case
- Title: “[Comp] vs. [Project]” + “Business plan to replicate [comp] success”.
- Two-column table (Comp base year vs Project). Rows: Acquisition price (€/psm), Buildability (sqm), Total investment (€/psm), ERV (€/psm/month), Exit price (€/psm), IRR/Multiple.
- Bullets “Business plan”: location, design/architect, works, certifications (LEED/BREEAM), leasing, final buyer.
- Include photo or placeholder of the comp if available.

## 5) Contact + disclaimer
- Sponsor details: address, email, web, contact person.
- Legal footer in small type (reuse from `assets/modelo-bolivia.pdf`).

## Optional extensions (add only if decision-relevant)
- Capex & timeline: gantt + breakdown (acq, demo, works, FF&E, fees).
- Capital stack: equity / mezz / senior with % and cost.
- Sensitivities: NOI vs yield, ERV ±5%, exit ±50 bps.
- ESG: certifications, energy upgrades, carbon footprint.
